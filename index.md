# Privacy Policy — Kasafonyini

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Kasafonyini ("the App") is a media player developed by Frimpong Emmanuel. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Data We Collect
The App does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data.
- No analytics
- No advertising identifiers
- No usage statistics
- No location data

## Permissions Explained
- READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO — Required to list your media files.
- RECORD_AUDIO — Used only for optional voice search.
- READ_PHONE_STATE — Used only to pause playback during calls.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Used to show playback controls in the notification.
- INTERNET — Required for network stream playback.
- WRITE_SETTINGS — Only if you choose to set an audio file as your ringtone.

## Data Storage
Playback positions, preferences, and playlists are stored locally on your device. This data is never transmitted.
