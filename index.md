Kasafonyini is a modern, lightweight application built by NexaByte Solutions to help you manage and play your local media files effortlessly. Enjoy a clean interface, fast performance, and a seamless user experience designed specifically for you.

Developed by NexaByte Solutions, we believe that great technology should be practical and accessible to everyone. We are dedicated to building high-quality digital tools that solve real problems for users in Ghana and around the world.

🎬 PLAYS EVERYTHING
• MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, FLV
• H.264, H.265/HEVC, VP8, VP9
• MP3, AAC, FLAC, Opus, AC3, DTS

📝 SUBTITLES THAT WORK
• Auto-loads embedded and external subtitles
• SRT, ASS, SSA, VTT format support
• Full encoding support (UTF-8, GBK, Shift-JIS)
• Adjustable font size and color

🌐 STREAM ANYTHING
• HTTP, HTTPS, HLS, DASH
• RTSP, RTMP, UDP, RTP
• Network shares (SMB)

🎨 DESIGNED FOR YOU
• Gesture controls (swipe for volume, brightness, seek)
• Picture-in-Picture mode
• Sleep timer & Resume playback
• Audio equalizer with presets

⚡ ENGINEERED FOR PERFORMANCE
• Hardware-accelerated decoding (up to 8K)
• Automatic software fallback for difficult files
• Zero ads. Zero tracking. Free forever.
